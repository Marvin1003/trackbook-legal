Trackbook support

A free training log for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to marvin.klaproth@gmail.com. Trackbook is a private project, so answers can take a few days. Please say which phone and app version you use (Settings, at the bottom).

Moving to a new phone

On an iPhone or iPad with iCloud sync switched on, choose From iCloud on the first screen of the new device, signed in with the same Apple ID. Otherwise export a backup in Settings › Your data, move the file to the new phone and choose Restore a backup on its first screen. On Android a backup file is the only way.

Coming from another app

Trackbook imports workout exports from Hevy, Strong, Fitbod and FitNotes: Settings › Your data › Import from another app. An import can be undone.

Deleting your data

There is no account. See Delete your data for the notebook on your phone and in iCloud.

Note

Trackbook is a training log, not a medical device. It gives no medical or health advice. You train at your own risk.
