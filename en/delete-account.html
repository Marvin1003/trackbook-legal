Delete your Trackbook account

Trackbook works without an account. If you made one to sync your notebook, you can delete it and everything stored in it at any time.

In the app

Open Settings and, in the Account section, tap Delete account and confirm. The account and the notebook stored in it are deleted at once. The notebook on your phone stays until you clear it (Settings › Clear notebook) or delete the app.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email marvin.klaproth@gmail.com from the address your account uses (for Sign in with Apple with "Hide my email", say so and we will find the account with you) and write "Delete my Trackbook account". We delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: account ID, email address and name.
  • Everything synced to it: workouts, sets and notes, routines, custom exercises, equipment, gyms, body-weight entries, and your name, unit and weekly goal.
  • Trackbook's access to your Apple ID, if you signed in with Apple.

Nothing is kept. Copies in database backups are overwritten within 30 days. Crash reports, if you switched them on, contain no account data and are deleted after 30 days anyway.
