Delete your Trackbook account

Trackbook works without an account. If you made one to sync your notebook, you can delete it and everything stored in it at any time.

In the app

Open Settings and, in the Account section, tap Delete account and confirm. The account and the notebook stored in it are deleted at once. The notebook on your phone stays until you clear it (Settings › Clear notebook) or delete the app.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email marvin.klaproth@gmail.com from the address your account uses (for Sign in with Apple with "Hide my email", say so and we will find the account with you) and write "Delete my Trackbook account". We delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: account ID, email address and, if your sign-in passed one on, your name.
  • Everything synced to it: workouts, sets and notes, routines, custom exercises, equipment, gyms, body-weight entries, workouts imported from other apps with the original rows of the imported file, and your name, unit, weekly goal and time zone.
  • The record of your consent to sync (which version of the text you agreed to, and when) and, if you subscribed to Trackbook Sync, its record: product, the store's transaction ID or purchase token, and expiry date.
  • Trackbook's access to your Apple ID, if you signed in with Apple. The app revokes it when you delete the account on your iPhone. If that is not possible (for example when we delete the account at your request by email), the app or our reply says so, and you remove Trackbook yourself in your Apple Account settings under Sign in with Apple.

Copies in database backups are overwritten within 30 days. Tax law may require us to keep records of payments we receive from the App Store or Google Play for up to ten years; they contain nothing from your notebook (see section 4 of the privacy policy). Crash reports, if you switched them on, contain no account data and are deleted after 30 days anyway. Nothing else is kept.
