Privacy policy

Trackbook app and this website · Version of 13 October 2026

Trackbook is a training log. It works without an account, and then your notebook never leaves your phone. This policy explains which data leaves the phone, when, why, and who receives it. The German version is the binding one.

1. Who is responsible

Marvin Klaproth
Email: marvin.klaproth@gmail.com

Trackbook is a private, non-commercial project. No data protection officer is required. For anything about your data, write to the address above.

2. Your notebook on the phone

Workouts, sets, routines, exercises, equipment, gyms, body weight, your name, unit and weekly goal are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. We cannot see them. They stay there until you clear the notebook in Settings or delete the app. You can export them any time as CSV or as a backup file (Settings › Your data). If your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) is switched on, the system includes the notebook in it, under Apple's or Google's terms. Data from Apple Health or Health Connect is kept out of it (section 3).

3. Apple Health and Health Connect (optional, off by default)

Only if you switch it on in Settings does Trackbook exchange data with Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) on your phone. Each part is switched on separately:

  • Workouts: Trackbook writes every finished workout (type, start, end, name) there. To avoid adding a workout your watch already recorded, the app reads other apps' workouts at the same time. Heart rate and active calories that a watch or chest strap measured during the workout are shown with it; these values are not stored. Trackbook writes no calories.
  • Body weight: Trackbook writes the weight you enter from then on, and reads weight from other apps (such as a scale) to show it in your profile.

This is health data (Art. 9 GDPR). It is processed only on your phone: data from Apple Health or Health Connect never goes to our servers, into sync or into Trackbook's backup files, and is neither shared nor used for advertising. Weights read from there, the record of what Trackbook wrote there and the Health switches are kept in a separate part of the app's storage that your phone's own backups (iCloud, Google or a computer) leave out, as does the transfer to a new Android phone, so on a phone set up from such a backup, Health is off until you switch it on there. Backups made before October 2026 can still contain them. Legal basis: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), given with the switch and the system's access dialog. You can withdraw it at any time: switch it off (Trackbook can then remove what it wrote), or revoke access in Apple Health or Health Connect. Weights read from there are deleted from the app when you switch it off. What Trackbook wrote to Apple Health or Health Connect is managed there, under Apple's or Google's terms.

4. Account and sync (optional)

What: when you sign in with Apple, we receive an account ID from Apple, your email address (or an anonymous Apple relay address if you choose "Hide my email") and, the first time, your name. With your consent, Trackbook then stores your notebook in your account: workouts, sets and notes, routines, custom exercises, equipment, gyms (with their location and address if you saved one), body-weight entries, workouts imported from other apps (including the original rows of the imported file), and your name, unit, weekly goal and time zone.

Why and legal basis: the account exists so you can use your notebook on several phones (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of the service you asked for). Training data and body weight can say something about your health, so we only upload the notebook with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The app asks for it before the first upload. Our server records which version of the consent text you agreed to and when, and also when you withdraw it, so we can prove it (Art. 7(1) GDPR). Without a current consent the server accepts no uploads.

Withdrawing consent: in Settings › Account, choose "Stop sync and delete cloud copy". This deletes the notebook stored in your account at once and signs this phone out; the notebook on your phone, your account and your subscription stay. You can also delete your account in Settings, or ask us by email (see Delete your account). Withdrawal does not affect anything done before it.

Recipient: Supabase, Inc. (supabase.com) operates the database for us as our processor (Art. 28 GDPR). The data is stored on servers in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, encrypted in transit and at rest. Only you can see it in the app; as the operator we could technically access it, and do so only if you ask us to or the law requires it. The notebook is not end-to-end encrypted.

Third countries: Supabase, Inc. is a US company. Supabase's data processing agreement contains the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). US authorities may be able to require US companies to hand over data.

Sign in with Apple: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Cork, Ireland, processes your Apple ID sign-in under its own responsibility. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

How long: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes the account and the notebook stored in it at once. Copies in the database backups are overwritten within 30 days. When you delete an account made with Apple, we also revoke Trackbook's access to your Apple ID.

Trackbook Sync subscription: when sync is a paid subscription, you buy it through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment under their own responsibility; we never see your payment details. Your account ID travels with the purchase to Apple or Google, so the purchase can only unlock your account. To check that the subscription is running, the app sends the store's transaction ID or purchase token to our server, which asks Apple or Google for its status and stores the product, that ID and the expiry date with your account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). These are deleted with your account; tax law may require us to keep records of payments we receive from the stores for up to ten years.

Age: you need to be 16 or older to create an account (Art. 8 GDPR).

5. Crash reports (optional, off by default)

If you switch on "Crash reports" on the first screen or in Settings, the app sends a report when it crashes: the error type, where in the code it happened, the app version, the operating system and the device model. For errors in the app's own code, the error message is removed before sending. A crash inside the system or native code can also include the system's error message, the phone's language, time zone, free memory and storage, and a random ID the crash reporter keeps for this installation. Workout values, notes, imported files and your name are never included. Sentry is set not to store your IP address. There are no screen recordings, traces, usage sessions or breadcrumbs.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Switching it off stops reports at once.

Recipient: Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry), 45 Fremont Street, 8th Floor, San Francisco, CA 94105, USA, as our processor, with data stored in the EU (Frankfurt). Sentry is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) and has agreed to the Standard Contractual Clauses. Reports are deleted after 30 days.

6. App updates

When it starts, the app asks Expo's update service whether a newer version of its code is available, and downloads it if so. The request contains your IP address, the platform, the app version and update channel, and a random ID for this installation that Expo uses to roll out updates. It contains nothing from your notebook.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in delivering bug fixes and security updates (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); this is strictly necessary for the service you use (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

Recipient: 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo), Palo Alto, California, USA, as our processor, based on the Standard Contractual Clauses. Expo keeps request logs for a limited time.

7. Gym map

When you open the gym map or search for gyms, the app loads map tiles and gym locations from OpenStreetMap-based services. They receive your IP address and the map area you are looking at, which can reveal roughly where you are. Your exact GPS position, your notebook and your account are not sent to these services. The map is only used when you open it; location permission is asked for only then, and you can use the map without it. While the map is open on Wi-Fi, the app also downloads the map around your starred gyms so it works offline; the tile service then sees those areas.

  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap (openfreemap.org)
  • Gym data, via the Overpass API: overpass-api.de (FOSSGIS e.V., Germany), overpass.private.coffee and overpass.kumi.systems (tried only if the first one doesn't answer)

Legal basis: providing the feature you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). These services act as independent controllers; their own privacy policies apply. Gyms found are cached on your phone.

Street addresses: gyms from the map use their OpenStreetMap address. When you save a gym you placed yourself, or the app placed one for you at the start of a workout, its street address is looked up once with your phone's address service, which Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) runs under its own responsibility; it receives the saved point (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

At the start of a workout: if you already allowed location, the app checks on your phone whether you are at one of your gyms, and saves your position for a gym that has none yet. Nothing else is sent for this than the address lookup above.

8. App stores and notifications

Downloading the app goes through Apple's App Store or Google Play, which process data under their own responsibility. Rest-timer alerts and the Live Activity are created on your phone; no push service is used.

Trackbook has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and does not sell or share data for marketing.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to correct it (Art. 16), to have it deleted (Art. 17), to restrict processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Most of this you can do yourself in the app: edit or delete entries, export your notebook, switch off crash reports, delete your account. Otherwise write to marvin.klaproth@gmail.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one in the German state where you live.

13. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). In the cloud, each notebook can only be read with the account it belongs to (row-level security). Your sign-in is kept in the phone's keychain or keystore.

14. Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. If a change affects what you consented to, the app asks you again.
